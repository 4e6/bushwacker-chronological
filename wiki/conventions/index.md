# Convention

* [Design forks are decided with the maintainer](design-forks-collaborative.md) - On consequential design choices, lay out the mechanics and honest trade-offs and let the maintainer choose before building.
* [Metered-API cost discipline](metered-api-cost-discipline.md) - Prefer the cheapest sufficient path for any metered third-party API; quantify cost before adding one; never silently exceed a free tier.
